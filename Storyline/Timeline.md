# Southern Lights — Timeline

**Span:** June 21, 2564 (Falkland Treaty / first landing in Antarctica) → 2812 (Long Night War / the trek to Concordia). 248 years.

**Canonical name for this span: the Second Interwar Period** — the interwar stretch between the two anti-robot wars: the War of Upper Earth (ended 2564, first) and the Long Night War (2812, second). Confirmed in the source GDD's `World_History_Reference.md` ("the Federation matured over the Second Interwar Period") and dated precisely in the file below. **The entire show takes place inside the Second Interwar Period** — that's the umbrella name for the whole thing, not one chapter of it.

**Structure:** Robot/Doll-anchored cast. Core POV characters are robots — ageless, present across the full span. Human characters cycle through generations around them (descendants, allies, rivals) as the centuries pass. This makes Southern Lights a prequel epic to *Inner Tepenia*, ending at (or just before) the point where the game's own "present day" begins.

**Ensemble scale (decided 2026-07-11):** no singular main character — closer to *Game of Thrones*, where roughly 60% of named characters carry "main" weight. The large existing roster in [`../Worldspace/Characters.md`](../Worldspace/Characters.md) (full-span robots, war-era figures, human dynasties) is well-suited to this — casting decisions should lean toward widening which named characters get real POV weight, not narrowing to one or two leads.

## Southern Lights inherits this beat sheet directly

`Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Timeline.md` in the InnerTepeniaGDD repo isn't just a fact list — it's a full beat sheet dated across the whole 2564–2812 span, built as a sibling to an equivalent file for the *First* Interwar Period (2083–2564, Upper Earth's own history). It's a **hybrid/fusion structure you synthesized yourself**, combining Blake Snyder's *Save the Cat*, James Scott Bell's *Plot and Structure*, John Truby's *The Anatomy of Story*, and Joseph Campbell's *The Hero's Journey*, adjusted to your own needs — not a straight application of any one of them.

**Decided:** Southern Lights adopts this beat sheet directly as its macro-structure. Season/episode breaks map onto its beats (Opening Image / Break into Two / Midpoint / Break into Three / Finale / Closing Image), and the show fills in the beats still marked TBD in the source rather than inventing a separate shape.

Everything in the table and beats below is pulled from that source file (as of its 2026-07-10 state). "Confirmed canon" = locked in the source GDD. "TBD" = open even there, meaning Southern Lights could be the one to actually decide it (subject to staying reconcilable with the game).

### The one big unresolved tension: when does the Tower actually get built?

The source file itself flags a live contradiction, **not yet resolved even in the game's own canon**: Amundsen Tower's completion is established elsewhere as **~2629–2639**, but this beat sheet tentatively places it at the **Midpoint (~2688, exactly 50%)** as a deliberate structural choice (a "false victory" — the Federation's greatest achievement, 124 years before it's destroyed). The two dates disagree by roughly 50 years and haven't been reconciled. **Southern Lights needs to either wait for the source to settle this, or make an independent call** — worth flagging since the Tower era is a strong candidate for a season centerpiece either way.

**For context, the Long Night War's own date moved twice during the source's development** before settling at 2812: originally a vague "early 2800s"/~2794–2804 range → shifted to ~2712 ("early 2700s") on 2026-07-03 → finally fixed to the single precise year 2812 on 2026-07-04, with the entire post-war timeline shifted accordingly. 2812 is the most recent, most authoritative reading — but it's worth knowing the date has real drafting history, in case it moves again.

### Everything else in the beat sheet is TBD — meaning open to Southern Lights to help define

The source file has placement *spans* (percentages of the era) for these beats but no actual events yet:
- **Theme Stated** (~5%), **Set-Up** detail (2564–~2576, presumably early city-founding), **Catalyst** (~10%)
- **Debate** (~2589–2614) — Tepenia's collective hesitation before committing to nationhood
- **Break into Two** (~2614) — source has **three live candidate shapes**, all involving a crisis proving isolated cities can't survive alone plus a generational handoff (founding generation, mostly human and aging/dying by ~50 years post-Treaty since human lifespans aren't extended here, giving way to exile-born Tepenians who never knew Upper Earth — robots are the one group spanning both generations):
  - **Shape A:** the crisis discredits isolationism outright; exile-born humans organize cross-city aid without waiting for permission and it works
  - **Shape B:** a founders'-council handoff to native-born leadership collides by accident with the crisis, forcing improvisation
  - **Shape C:** the long-lived robots — not the humans — are the ones who push for federation, using centuries of institutional memory the newly-adult humans don't have
- **Road of Trials / Fun and Games** (~2614–2688) — known infrastructure sequence (cities → subnet highways → Arcanet subnet-by-subnet → Hwy 22 → Tower construction in its final 12–17 years) needs dramatizing; **B-Story = the Arcanet's construction**, converging with the Tower plot at the Midpoint
- **Kick in the Shins** — an early setback, explicitly **not** Tower-construction-related (ruled out); two candidates: losing whoever made Break into Two's federated response work, or an early infrastructure failure (a highway segment or Arcanet subnet connection failing outright)
- **Bad Guys Close In** (~2688–2750) — cracks beneath the Federation's confident surface
- **All Is Lost** (~2750), **Dark Night of the Soul** (~2750–2762)
- **Finale** (~2762–2812) — the ~50-year escalation from the Palmer City killing to the actual outbreak of war; source flags this gap as long enough to need real development, not left as a given
- **Closing Image** (~99–100%) — Tepenia at its most confident, immediately before the war destroys it
