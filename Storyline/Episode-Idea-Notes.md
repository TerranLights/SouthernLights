# Episode Idea Notes

Story ideas from a brainstorm for interim novels (2026-10-31). All of them fall inside the show's span, from the Falkland Treaty (2564) to the Long Night War (2812). The author confirmed that most of them are already set to be part of the series. Each one has a robot lead.

---

### The Crossing (2564)
The first exiles, robots with their human partners and supporters, cross to Antarctica and found Palmer City on the day the Falkland Treaty is signed.

### The Last Haul (~2614)
Robot truckers carry the last encrypted datadrives out of Pergamino (the old Juan Carlos), deep into the continent, to the new national archive at Amundsen Station.

### Across the Weddell (~2614)
A robot radio crew works to make contact between the Peninsula and Belgrano reliable. When they succeed, the country's networks join, and a crisis follows.

### The Transcontinental
The crews who build Hwy 22 from Byrd across the continent, through the South Pole, to the Zhongshan coast.

### Amundsen Tower (~2671–2688)
A robot construction chief raises the space fountain at the South Pole, the nation's proudest achievement.
- Ties in with Vosora, who organized the Tower's construction logistics (see `Writers-Room-Hooks.md`, "Amundsen Tower's fall, witnessed three ways").

### Rastra (after 2688)
A robot tug pilot in orbit, flying the small craft that inherited the old haulers' name.

### The Airbase That Never Stood Down
Belgrano's robot flight medics fly rescue runs across the ice, and their work becomes the Belgrano Institute of Medicine.

### Machu Picchu Customs
A robot officer at the national border checkpoint deals with arrivals from Upper Earth, the defectors among them, plus smugglers and spies.

### Palmer City Nights
A crime story in the jazz-and-casino capital, with a robot casino security chief as the lead. It ends with the killing in Palmer City that sets off the Long Night War.
- Ties in with Akina (SE-031) and the diplomat she kills in self-defense, c. 2762 (see `Writers-Room-Hooks.md`, "The Long Night War's actual spark").

### The Last Lift (2812)
A robot stays behind to keep Amundsen Tower running so the evacuees can get out, until the Tower is destroyed.
- Ties in with Kendra, who held the line at the Tower's base as it came down, and with the evacuation night (see `Writers-Room-Hooks.md`, "Amundsen Tower's fall, witnessed three ways" and "Who stayed, and who left").
