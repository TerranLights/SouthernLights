# Characters — Inherited from InnerTepeniaGDD

Source: `Worldspace/Characters/` in the InnerTepeniaGDD repo. Compiled 2026-07-10. Tags: **[SIP 2564–2812]** = falls inside the show's window, usable directly. **[POST-WAR/GAME-PRESENT ≈2822+]** = downstream consequence, belongs to *Inner Tepenia*'s own setting — useful only as "what this leads to." **[UNDATED]** = no era fixed anywhere in the source — open territory to originate.

Robots (Dolls) don't age biologically — components wear and need maintenance, but there's no biological clock. Any Doll built before or during the Second Interwar Period is a legitimate candidate to anchor Southern Lights across its full 248-year span. See [`Robot-Biology-and-Culture.md`](Robot-Biology-and-Culture.md) for how that actually works physically.

## Full-span robots — present at or before 2564, confirmed alive ≈2822+

**Maggie Aarden** — Byrd · SP 5w6 ("The Castle") — **[SIP 2564]**
The Treaty's actual robot signatory. A Midwestland industrial-yard worker and Steampunk artist who, after rising anti-robot sentiment cost her career, developed a quiet third-option resettlement plan for her community (neither fighting nor submitting) — a pre-war effort that the War of Upper Earth then escalated into full leadership over the robot population at large, though she never sought or caused the war itself. That war-forged credibility is exactly why **the Assembly Powers (Upper Earth's own governments)** "invited her to volunteer" to sign at Stanley: they'd already chosen her before consulting her, precisely because her duty-bound SP 5w6 psychology made refusal nearly impossible once framed as duty, and a credible robot leader's signature made the exile look consented-to rather than simply imposed on the defeated. **Corrected 2026-07-11: the pressure came from Upper Earth's governments, not from her own robot community, as an earlier draft of this doc had it.** Settled first in Rothera, then permanently in Byrd. Alive in the game's present, central figure of the game's Byrd DLC. **This is the show's founding wound — see [`../Storyline/The-Falkland-Treaty.md`](../Storyline/The-Falkland-Treaty.md).**

**Virgo Deep Level Keeper** — district NPC (Undergrid) — **[SIP pre-2564]**
Built in the decade *before* the Federation formally existed — physically helped construct Concordia's original "Deep Level" infrastructure before the Federation's founding, then maintained/guarded it continuously for over 250 years. Her frame "predates the Federation engineering standards entirely." Very likely the single oldest active Doll in the entire dataset — literally older than Tepenia as a nation. Strongest candidate in the whole dataset for a Southern Lights robot-anchor or direct crossover figure with the game.

**Ji-Eun Kim** — Aquarius ("The Labs") · 5w4 Social — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Unified Korea nanotech engineer, already one of the greatest in the world pre-2500, fighting professional exclusion from anti-robot sentiment before the War of Upper Earth. Caught in the Falkland Treaty's blanket robot provision and exiled 2564-06-21 (not personally named — "simply a robot, and that was sufficient"); first landing at Sejong → relocated to Zhongshan → eventually Concordia. Built her own nanotech testing facility (now ruins); engineered the Eyes of Gold's signature gold ocular prosthetics without ever joining them. Vanished before/during the Long Night War — a deliberate choice, using self-engineered nanotech concealment, for three interlocking reasons: the concealment tech was originally a gift for someone who died before receiving it; it was the first thing she ever built purely for herself; the concealment is designed to be permanent and now traps her the way it once freed her. Calethina does not know she's alive — confirmed core fact. Alive, hidden in Aquarius, in the game's present.

**Nilda Faviola "Favi" della Torre** — Taurus / Eyes of Gold · 6w5 Self-Preservational — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Built in Italy as a lab assistant to an elderly scientist grieving his own daughter's death; he came to love Favi as a surrogate daughter. Exiled in 2564 — too old/frail to follow her south, he died soon after from compounded grief; she only learns this generations later via a datashard. Joined the Eyes of Gold after falling in love with a fellow (human) member — her "anchor." During the Long Night War, her boyfriend was captured by Upper Earth forces; she went in alone with a sniper rifle and a dagger, killed everyone in the compound, and found him already dead. Still active as Taurus's community security anchor, "ready to take up the gun again."

**Trisha Miller** — Taurus · 8w7 Social/Assertive — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Midwestland radio DJ hosting a commute rock-'n'-roll program during a period of high human-robot tension. Forcibly removed from her studio the moment the Falkland Treaty was signed and exiled — two human coworkers/close friends chose to follow her voluntarily into exile. First settled in Belgrano; survived the Long Night War; relocated to Concordia afterward, where she resumed broadcasting. Her two human friends have both since died — she outlived them "by decades" and doesn't discuss it on air. Runs "The Signal" out of Taurus in the game's present — written explicitly as a Social 8w7 who remembers the Falkland Treaty, the Long Night War, and everything in between, "carries long-duration memory of Concordia's political and community history from a ground-level perspective. What she knows and what official records say may not be the same thing."

**Michelle Stanton** — Gemini (Information district) · 5w6 Social — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Built as a construction robot in Midwestland, exiled 2564 — same generational cohort as Ayako and Ji-Eun. One of the literal builders of the Arcanet's physical infrastructure across all six regional subnets, splitting time pre-war between resting in nearby cities and travelling the frozen wastes in a "Rastra" all-terrain vehicle. After the Long Night War, chose permanent Concordia residence maintaining city infrastructure — despite uniquely retaining a working means of continental travel she never uses to leave. Co-investigates "the Great Corruption" with Vosora Lashár Tanslock, verifying what wasn't destroyed while Vosora reconstructs what was.

**Vosora Lashár Tanslock** — Gemini (Information district) · 5w6 Social — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Tepenia-native, pre-war nomadic logistics/data-management specialist with no fixed hometown — "allegiance was never to a place, always to the work." Her largest pre-war project: she personally organized the logistics, data supply chains, and command structures for the planning and construction of **Amundsen Tower** — decades before its destruction. Happened to be in Concordia when the Long Night War began and was trapped there ever since. Investigates the Great Corruption from the reconstruction side; the trail connects to Libra's "Suspended Compact" and possibly the Falkland Treaty legacy itself.

**Griselda Alejandra Chávez** — off-world (Deimos) · 6w5 Self-Preservational — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Sonora-built blacksmith. The Treaty's exile provision applied the moment it was signed, but word didn't reach Sonora immediately — she arrived in Tepenia some time after. Settled in Belgrano; became an instrument-maker specializing in pianos using whalebone in place of wood. Shipped instruments across the Weddell Sea to Esperanza, feeding Palmer City's concert culture. Caught an early ride up Amundsen Tower during the Long Night War evacuation (not a last-minute escape); now runs a scrapyard/trading business on Deimos. Personally knew Salagéa Aparast pre-war in Belgrano. Loves humans and robots equally.

**Leo Cymaticist Elder** — district NPC — **[SIP 2590s–present]** — earliest Federation founding cohort; developing a "frequency theory" experiment on and off since before the Long Night War interrupted it.

**Cancer Unlimited Elder Caregiver** — district NPC — **[SIP ~2580s–present]** — one of the first integration facilitators after the Treaty. Present for the 2813 Overcrowding Decision; made undisclosed choices there she still carries alone.

**Taurus Cold-Side Representative** — district NPC — **[SIP ~2640s–present]** — active since Taurus's founding decade; outlived her original bonded family by 30 years; keeps 60 years of personally recorded thermal data.

**Pisces "Dying Robot"** — district NPC — **[SIP early 2630s–present]** — direct pre-war memories of Federation city life before destruction; encountered early Calethina "when she was newly singular." Bonded 60 years to human partner Lío (now 81); dying, racing to pass on her pre-war sensory memories before she goes. One of the richest "last living link to the founding era" figures in the whole dataset — the show could simply *be* those memories.

## War-era figures — defined by the Long Night War (2812)

**Akina [SE-031]** — Tepenia native · Palmer City — **[SIP ~2762]**
**Confirmed 2026-07-12: the Long Night War's inciting figure.** Genuinely amicable and hospitable, happy to entertain and help humans in good faith by default — with a sharp break into sudden, lethal decisiveness once a real line is crossed. Working in Palmer City's nightlife/entertainment economy when, c. 2762, an Upper Earth diplomat (never named in canon) denies her personhood and attempts to assault her; she kills him in self-defense — the single event that commits Tepenia irreversibly toward the Long Night War (Break into Three, ~80% of the era — see [`../Storyline/Timeline.md`](../Storyline/Timeline.md)). Confirmed to survive through the war and into the game's present (~2822–2827) as a companion character — meaning she carries the full weight of "the person who started the war" across the entire remaining 50-year span of the show and beyond. Origin city undecided among three candidates: Zukelli (leading candidate, not locked), Dumont d'Urville, or Byrd. The ~50-year gap between her incident and the war's actual outbreak is unwritten — open territory for the show. (Not to be confused with "Solenne," a separate, unrelated Palmer City figure with her own unconnected unresolved incident.)

**Kendra Heinrich** — South Pole · 8w7 Social — **[SIP 2812]**
From Upper Earth (exact origin TBD). During the final evacuations before Amundsen Tower's destruction, held the line at the Tower's base against Upper Earth military forces while evacuees caught the last rides up. The strike likely hit the accelerator/power complex, not her directly — ~246,000 tonnes of the tower collapsed over several minutes, landing almost exactly on her position. Survived, likely in the underground foundation shaft, and has been trapped/stranded under the wreckage ever since — "having spent so much of her life fighting to protect others, now she's the one who needs rescuing." A "war goddess" outlier by design (all four core stats maxed). Unbreakable loyalty rule once bonded: turns instantly hostile if anyone harms innocent/defenseless people.

**Eirwyn "Eira" Cardoss** — off-world/MIA (last known: South America) — **[SIP War of Upper Earth – pre-2812]**
Gynoid iceblade assassin. Believes in robot sovereignty above all else; "has no qualms about 'removing' any human who tries to harm a robot." Post-War-of-Upper-Earth, regularly went into Upper Earth lands to eliminate threats to Tepenia. Caught and imprisoned; her human guard came to agree with her cause, fell in love, helped her escape — becoming a "traitor" in Upper Earth's eyes. He fought alongside her until he died before the Long Night War (cause undetermined). Status: MIA, presumed alive.

**Zhuldyz Serikbolatkyzy** — off-world/MIA (now: Mars) — **[SIP War of Upper Earth – 2812]**
Gynoid built in Kazakhstan for **Turar Serikbolat**, sole heir to the family farm his father spent his life savings to buy her for. They "fell deeply, madly in love." Escaped to Antarctica together during the War of Upper Earth; she applied her mechanical expertise to salvage work in Tepenia. **What happened to Turar during the Tepenia years and the Long Night War is explicitly flagged as "the most significant open question"** in her story — whether he escaped off-world with her is not established. She escaped via Amundsen Tower, now runs a scrapyard on Mars. A ready-made doomed-or-not romance spanning the entire show.

**Seica Cenilaithe** — Scorpio · 8w7 Sexual — **[SIP 2812]**
Goth robot, Tepenia native. Her human husband, whom she "loved deeply," was killed during the Long Night War. Carries contained, principled hatred toward Upper Earth governments/militaries — no reachable target, "so it lives inside her." Companion arc "The One I Couldn't Stop" explores what happens when something finally reachable appears.

**Villena Hiresvett** — Leo · 7w6 Self-Preservational — **[SIP 2812]**
Tepenia native from Belgrano, moved to Palmer City chasing fame as a rockabilly/electroswing performer. Missed the last ride to Amundsen Tower and **personally watched it be destroyed in front of her**; fled to Concordia instead, arriving as a war refugee. Recurring refrain: "she will never see the stars" (Concordia is enclosed) and will never achieve national fame because "there is no longer a nation; just a city." Genuine, unchanged love of her music before and after.

**Garcelle [G.A.R. – Cell E]** — off-world/MIA — **[SIP 2812]** and **[POST-WAR, well past 2827]**
"G.A.R." = Garrison for AGI Relocation, Division 14, Cell E. Refused to evacuate, survived the war in hiding, unknown to Concordia. 8w9 Self-Preservational — territorial; loyal exclusively to robots, "believes the only good human is a dead human." **Note:** her most extreme material (capture, torture, forced identity erasure, forcible ejection from Earth) is explicitly stated to occur long after the game's own events — usable only as an epilogue/flash-forward, not core show content.

**Nieza Yúnuri** — off-world/MIA (now: Mars) — **[SIP 2812]**
Gynoid with a severe fear of heights, and a greater fear of human violence — that ordering drove her onto one of the very last rides up Amundsen Tower, forcing herself on despite the fear because the alternative was worse. "Whatever happened at the base of the Amundsen Tower in its final hours — she saw it." Otherwise a blank slate, flexible for invention.

**Majyao Bisyugota** — Taurus (teahouse keeper) · 4w5 Self-Preservational — **[SIP 2812]**
From Janbogo; owned a famous teahouse there sourcing exotic/smuggled teas, including "Blood River Tea" from near Blood Falls. Deliberately chose not to flee to Amundsen Tower — stayed and rebuilt in Concordia; the war permanently cut her tea supply line. Teahouse functions as neutral ground across factions.

**Salagéa Aparast** — Halley Subnet, Atlantic coast near Belgrano · 1w2 Social — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
**Corrected 2026-07-11 — not settled in Cancer/Concordia; an earlier draft of this doc had that wrong.** Tepenia native from Belgrano. Lives alone on a self-sailed boat working the Atlantic coastal region, an itinerant courier rather than a fixture of any settled community — she does not appear in Concordia. Retrieves datashards from the pre-war Upper Earth internet and delivers them throughout Tepenia for Arcanet archiving, evading Upper Earth hostility. During the Long Night War, deliberately chose not to flee via Amundsen Tower — stayed to keep cataloguing and delivering knowledge to those still on the surface. Personally knew Griselda Alejandra Chávez pre-war in Belgrano (Griselda evacuated early — off-screen relationship). Belgrano itself survived the war intact and declined afterward; Salagéa's disciplined, purposeful courier work reads as a direct expression of that city's founding Air Force ethos, even living apart from it. (Her prior connection to Cancer's 2813 Overcrowding Decision has been retracted — no such link is confirmed.)

## Pre-Treaty figures — founding-myth material, mostly undeveloped past 2564

All three predate the Falkland Treaty by centuries with essentially no documented presence after it — wide-open territory, but carries real founding-myth weight.

**Doris Morikawa** — Head Librarian, Hall of Archives, Sovereign Republic of Hawaii · 2w3 Social — **[UNDATED, pre-2564]**
Cataloged/preserved the entirety of pre-war human knowledge at a politically neutral zone. What happened to her and the archives during the two wars is completely undetermined — open hook: did the Hall of Archives survive, potentially as a third source of truth alongside the shattered subnet archives left by the Split Brain?

**Hana Jinn** — Data Analytics/Research Expert, U.R.U.K. → A.R.U.Ta.G. · 4w3 Self-Preservational — **[UNDATED, pre-2564]**
Worked at U.R.U.K. (where the first sentient robot was built) developing metamaterials for mass drivers — the earliest engineering groundwork for what became the Amundsen Tower. A great lover of whalesong. Whether she survived to the Falkland Treaty is unknown. Central irony: "a robot who may have died before the Long Night War helped make the escape possible for those who survived it."

**Mallory Dufay** — Structural/Safety Inspector, the CSA (Texas) — **[UNDATED, pre-2564]**
Active career placed closer to the mid-2300s — oversaw the actual first launches and construction of orbital infrastructure, following Hana Jinn's research phase. Framed as "a quiet epic" of unglamorous heroism. Whether she survived to the Falkland Treaty era is undetermined.

## Other still-present Dolls — thinner or purely present-day material

**Calethina** — archive-AI, not a physical Doll (holographic projection, based at Concordia's Engineering & Activation Lab, sited at the Cancer/Taurus/Capricorn three-district corner) — **[SIP pre-2812–present]**
**Backstory resolved via a real design session, 2026-07-12** (superseding the earlier unreviewed AI-drafted questline draft this doc previously flagged as provisional). Has been present since before the war and helped coordinate the Amundsen Tower evacuation; largely isolated in her lab since. Her degradation is tied to a specific, confirmed cause: the same catastrophic energy-grid mega-shock that caused the Planetary Split Brain also partially corrupted her — not an isolated malfunction unique to her. Degrading holograph shows periodic glitches/echoes of older versions of herself, and she sometimes confuses recent events with war-era memories. Her own lab's fabrication chamber — which activates newly-sentient robots there — was itself built at Mountain Pass (a since-defunct Vostok/Kunlun joint venture) and shipped via a seven-stop route across six subnets plus the South Pole before installation; Mountain Pass's manufacturing ended permanently in the same Tower collapse that caused her own degradation — one catastrophe, two converging consequences at the same lab. Her long-term survival remains a genuinely open question as a matter of backstory (not scoped further here — companion/romance/re-embodiment mechanics are the game's own domain). Still open independent of her own arc: Ji-Eun Kim's ultimate fate, which Calethina's knowledge of the ruined nanotech facility references but doesn't resolve.

**Ayako Hayashi** — medic, Red Spiral, Cancer · 4w5 Self-Preservational — **[SIP 2564–2812]**
Activated in Japan; exiled 2564. Pre-exile: artist/fashion figure, severe depression, found escape in Schopenhauer's aesthetic philosophy. In the Tepenia years, the human man she loved died in an accident she witnessed and couldn't prevent — reshaped her into a medic. Lost many close friends in the Long Night War; settled in Concordia.

**Naizelle d'Edjordoś** — scavenger/community anchor, Pisces · 5w6 Self-Preservational — **[SIP Tepenia-native]**
Born/built within Tepenia itself — her whole existence sits inside the show's window. Deeply embedded in Tepenia's Heavy Metal/Industrial scene pre-war. The Long Night War destroyed her home city (specific city TBD); fled to Concordia/Pisces, began salvaging to survive, unintentionally built a stable hab compound.

**Lyuba "Lyubochka" Baranova** — Aries, unarmed fighter · 8w7 Sexual — **[SIP built "not long before the Long Night War"]**
From East Intermaria (formerly West Russia), one of the youngest Dolls in the roster. Keeps 8–12 pre-exile paper books of undisclosed provenance, given away only as a post-romance reward.

**Elva [TCY-06] and Lillian [TCY-42]** — Leo performers, opposing houses — **[SIP mid-2740s]** — living embodiments of Leo's "Star War" (see [`Locations.md`](Locations.md)); Elva on the winning house, Lillian on the losing, still-resentful "legacy" house.

**Thinner/undeveloped:** IT-068 "Flora" (Capricorn tradesrobot, war involvement TBD), Meyzan Yocazhda (Leo, undeveloped), and a ~13-strong remaining "TBN" sketch-stage roster (Pink Lucy — settled in Janbogo via Dumont d'Urville, whether she later reached Concordia or stayed through the war is open; Fenny, Miranda, Rui, Momo, Mei-Li, Charlene, Angelina, and an unnamed template) — mostly visual-concept-only, near-zero historical content, open territory. (Akina has been promoted to her own entry above — see War-era figures.)

## Human characters, dynasties, and district figures

**Taurus Established Council Representative** — human, late 60s — **[SIP 2564 →]**
Five-generation Taurus family, descended from "first bonded families to establish in the district after the Falkland Treaty." The clearest ready-made human dynasty template for a show whose human cast needs to cycle through generations around a persistent robot lead.

**Aries Safety Engineer's family** — multi-generation lineage organized around an ancestor's disputed death — the family calls it "preventable," the official record calls it "acceptable operational loss." An institutional-grievance dynasty.

**Capricorn Merit Establishment Foreman's family** — three-generation guild lineage, grandfather "a guild member in Capricorn's early years" — founding-era roots.

**Libra Pragmatist Council Chair** — human, early 60s — **[SIP → POST-WAR]** — found the "Suspended Compact" 40 years before game-present; suppressed for "two hundred and thirty years," i.e. since essentially the Federation's founding.

**Scorpio Memory Reconstruction Patient** — human, late 50s — **[SIP 2812]** — was a Libra council aide *during the Long Night War itself*, present in real time for evacuation-priority decisions; now seeking to have those memories erased.

**Upper Earth Defectors** (faction, not named individuals) — **[SIP 2812 → POST-WAR]** — military defectors who arrived in Concordia as refugees after the war and the Tower evacuation. Three subfactions: **The Remorseful** (genuine guilt), **The Pragmatics** (survival-focused, "just following orders"), **The Infiltrators** (active saboteurs). Close to a ready-made "what happened right after the show ends" bible.

## Design note on lineage

Several long-lived Dolls function as *de facto* lineage-substitutes, since robots don't reproduce generationally but persist across the whole 248-year span: Ji-Eun Kim, Favi della Torre, Trisha Miller, the Cancer Elder Caregiver, the Leo Cymaticist Elder, the Taurus Cold-Side Representative, and above all the Virgo Deep Level Keeper and the Pisces "Dying Robot." The source GDD has independently arrived at exactly the device Southern Lights already assumes: robots as memory-carriers spanning what for humans would be multiple generations.
