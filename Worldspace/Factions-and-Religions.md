## The six robot religions

**Ice-Cold Buddhism** (placeholder name) — **[SIP, ~2 centuries of documented doctrinal development]**
Grounded in real physics: as electron-conducting matter cools, electrons approach superconductor velocity; at a threshold, any computation-capable matter/device temporarily becomes a superconductor. For a robot, achieving this state reads as something like meditative nirvana — a robot-native analog to enlightenment. Two holy sites, whose doctrinal rivalry runs explicitly across two centuries of the Second Interwar Period: **Kunlun** (higher, colder, harder to reach — "devotion is earned through purpose," population curated from Tepenia's space/astronomy/communications-heritage nations) vs. **Dome Fuji** (Tepenia's highest-elevation city, ~3,810m — "devotion requires nothing but arrival," uncurated, drawn from every Tepenian city; founded from an abandoned secular Japanese ice-core research mission whose original founders departed entirely right after the Falkland Treaty, leaving the site unoccupied for 1–3 decades until robot-only pilgrims rediscovered and resettled it). Rare travel between the two sites keeps the disagreement alive without ever producing a schism. Note: Southern Lights' title comes from the aurora generally, not specifically from Dome Fuji's vision notes — the two are coincidentally adjacent in the source material, not causally linked.

**Cymatics Reverence** — **[UNDATED — open for the show to originate]**
Reveres sound/vibration — not mundane acoustic levitation, but the larger, deliberately unresolved claim that controlled vibration of spacetime itself could move planets. Whether this is real science-in-progress or purely sacred/non-literal is explicitly flagged as a genuinely load-bearing choice the developer hasn't made yet. Robots perceive sound/vibration across a meaningfully wider range than humans (see [`Robot-Biology-and-Culture.md`](Robot-Biology-and-Culture.md)) — read here as devotion, not research. Humans may join but are described as joining a practice built on a perceptual reality "not fully theirs to share." No single home city, unlike Ice-Cold Buddhism's paired holy sites.

**The Eyes of Gold** — **[POST-WAR/GAME-PRESENT operational form; SIP origin via named individuals]**
Core belief: James Bond was a real historical intelligence operative; "Goldeneye" is reinterpreted literally — his targeting ability came from a golden ocular prosthetic. Central rite of passage: surgical replacement of a fully-trusted operative's dominant eye with a gold implant, engineered by Ji-Eun Kim (never a member). Two Dolls with SIP-era backstories (Favi della Torre, Ji-Eun Kim) tie the faction directly into the show's window even though its documented Concordia-wide operational structure is game-present.

**Sylvester James Gates Adinkras** (placeholder name) — **[UNDATED — brainstorm only]** — oriented around Universal Simulation Theory; speculated nihilism subdivisions modeled on Camus, Sartre, Nietzsche. No practices, founders, adherents, or city/era attribution.

**Polydimensional Animism** (placeholder name) — **[UNDATED — brainstorm only]** — belief that real living entities exist in higher dimensions beyond 3D perceptual range. No practices, founders, adherents, or era/city given.

**God-mind Universe Simulation** (placeholder name) — **[UNDATED — least developed]** — "God knows everything, except where God comes from. Therefore, God is constantly running simulations of his beloved creation in order to try to figure out where He Himself comes from." One line of concept, nothing else written.

## City-origin factions of the Second Interwar Period

The single most directly usable document in the source GDD for the show's everyday texture — explicitly framed as covering "the roughly 200-year span between the Falkland Treaty (2564) and the Long Night War (2812)," living/functioning cities, not displaced populations. **All entries below are [SIP 2564–2812].**

| **Troll, Davis, Mawson, Abowasa, Mirny, Sanay, Dumont d'Urville** *(+ dissenter Denison)* | Competence Without Commentary — seven cities converge on "demonstrated competence, not talk, is the currency of belonging," each with its own inflection (leverage, humility, inheritance, intimacy management, threshold-stewardship, refusal of exceptionalism, negotiation with the non-negotiable). ## Post-war refugee factions — the natural finale/epilogue layer

**[POST-WAR/GAME-PRESENT — these only make sense from 2812 onward, don't reframe to earlier Tepenian history.]** "Real people processing loss, displacement, and identity in a city that isn't home, decades into the current game era (≈2822–2827)."

- **The Continuity Schism** (Mirny) — The Continuants (keep practicing old schedules) vs. The Rupturists (those who stayed in Mirny are the ones actually still fighting for it).
- **The Burning Witness** (Zukelli) — The Witnesses (memory as moral obligation), The Separatists (quiet insularity), The Integrationists (Zukelli as part of identity, not the whole).
- **The Boneyard Times** (Belgrano) — "the city that survived the war did not survive the peace." Those who remember peak Belgrano vs. "the Boneyarders" born into decline.
- **The Question of the Unbroken Name** (Zukelli, Belgrano, Palmer City, Casey) — when does a person stop being "from" a destroyed city and become simply Tepenian?
- **The McMurdo Problem** (Fort McMurdo, Esperanza) — communities of lost function. Institutionalists (the tradition lives on wherever applied) vs. Specificists (decontextualized knowledge isn't really McMurdo anymore).

## Game-present-day-only factions (flagged, not detailed — postdate the show)

Presuppose Concordia already exists as a fully built-out post-2812 city, or form specifically because people "become refugees in Concordia" (strictly post-2812): 29 refugee affinity-group archetypes (The Frost-Hardened, The Tide-Kin, The Rememberers/Scar-Bearers, etc.); The Veilkeepers (guard/suppress pre-war data about the Falkland Treaty); The Vigil (maintains memorials to the destroyed Space Elevator and off-world evacuees); The Treaty Scholars (academics obsessed with the Treaty's consequences); plus The Lattice, The Eternal Winter Collective/Frost Ascetics, The Reclaimers, The Siligel Purists, The Neon Nomads/Highway Clans, The Chorus of the Deep, The Memory Weavers, The Iron Gardeners, The Quiet Shift, The Last Builders.
