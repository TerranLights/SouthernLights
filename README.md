# Southern Lights

Design documents for **Southern Lights**, a sci-fi WebTV series set in Tepenia, the Antarctic nation of robots and the humans who chose to live together with them.

## The series
- **Its span:** the show effectively **is** the Second Interwar Period, front to back: from the exiles stepping onto the shores of Antarctica (21 June 2564) to the Long Night War (2812). It is a prequel to the *Inner Tepenia* CRPG and ends where the game's own present begins.
- **An ensemble, not a hero.** There is no single main character and no single robot lead: think *Game of Thrones* or *Lost*, but science fiction. Many characters carry the story's weight, and the true protagonist is an **idea** rather than any one person (which idea is left to emerge as the storyline develops).
- **Robots and time.** The robots at the heart of the cast live far longer than humans, but they are not immortal: they can die, so a robot need not be present for the whole span. Human characters pass through generations around them as the centuries go by.
- **Shape:** the seasons follow the beat sheet of the Second Interwar Period (the Opening Image is the exiles' landing, a Midpoint at the completion of Amundsen Tower, the Long Night War as the finale; the final season shows the war on screen). The number of seasons is not decided yet; the current leaning is about sixteen.
- **Production:** AI-rendered and voice-acted by human actors.

## What this repo holds
Episode outlines, the series bible, casting material, production material, and the story and world reference the show is written from.

**Scripts are never tracked.** They are kept off GitHub on purpose, to protect the work; `.gitignore` excludes any `Scripts/` folder and screenplay file types.

## Layout

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `Storyline/` | The show's timeline and its beats; the Falkland Treaty as background (the show opens with the exiles' landing; the signing itself belongs to *The Saga of Maggie Aarden*); Amundsen Tower; the strongest dramatic material found so far (`Writers-Room-Hooks.md`); the weapons-and-tools philosophy; and episode ideas with their development notes |
| `Worldspace/` | Characters; factions and religions; locations; robot biology and culture; core systems; and environment references for Concordia's districts and the Federation's cities, for scene and set planning |
| `Reference/` | A map into the shared universe's source repos, and the facts most likely to matter for the show |

## Canon
The show shares one universe with every other Tepenian work. Southern Lights does not draw from any other work: it and every other work, the *Inner Tepenia* CRPG included, draw from the same **foundational data**, which governs them all. That data lives in the repos where it most logically belongs:
- **[Tepenian Universe Timeline](https://github.com/TerranLights/TepenianUniverseTimeline)**: the cross-media canon, the eras and the universal design laws. **Where any document here differs from it, the Timeline governs.** Several documents here date from July 2026 and predate later rulings (for example, Amundsen Tower is a space fountain completed about 2688; there are 38 cities in six subnets plus Amundsen Station).
- **[Derivation Mechanisms](https://github.com/TerranLights/DerivationMechanisms)**: the methods used to build characters, stories and places.
- **[Culture Library](https://github.com/TerranLights/CultureLibrary)**: the cultures of Tepenia's cities and of the nations its people came from.

Much of that data is still being drafted in the [Inner Tepenia](https://github.com/TerranLights/InnerTepeniaGDD) design repo, the current working ground. Once a piece of data is fully established, it moves to the repo where it belongs, and the works (this show and the game alike) draw it from there.
